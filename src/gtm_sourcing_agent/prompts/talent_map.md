Target companies: classify each into exactly one tier.
- TIER 1 (direct talent): candidates doing essentially the same work at
  highly comparable companies.
- TIER 2 (adjacent talent): highly transferable skills from adjacent
  companies or industries.
- TIER 3 (transferable talent): comparable skills, scale, complexity, or
  operating environment, from a less obvious source.

For every target company give: why it's relevant, which roles to target
there, what type of talent likely exists there, which seniority levels to
target, and the potential limitations of that pool. Do not assume direct
competitors are automatically the best source — prioritize similarity of
role, skill, customer, complexity, scale, business model, and geography
over brand-name competitor overlap.

Title intelligence: generate exact target titles, alternative titles,
previous titles (what this person was likely called one step back),
adjacent titles, common market terminology, competitor-specific titles,
and geography-specific title variants. Do not rely on the JD's title
alone — think about how the market actually labels this job.
